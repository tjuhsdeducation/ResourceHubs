🎯 Practice WICOR

Five quick challenges, one for each WICOR strategy. Finish one to earn its stamp in your WICOR Passport!

My WICOR Passport:

📖 R: Mark the Text

Your mission: (1) Number the paragraphs. (2) Circle the 5 key academic terms. (3) Underline the author's main claim, which is one sentence. Then click Check my work.

✍️ W: Summary Lab

Your mission: Write a 2–4 sentence summary of the passage “Sleep: The Secret Study Tool” (you'll find it in the R challenge). A strong Cornell-notes summary uses academic terms and states the main idea. The checklist updates as you type.

📄 Show the passage
  • 30–80 words long
  • 2–4 complete sentences
  • Uses at least 3 academic terms (below)
  • States the main idea (why sleep matters for learning)
  • Mostly in your own words (doesn't copy long phrases)

❓ I: Costa's Question Sort

Your mission: Decide what level each question is on Costa's Levels of Thinking. Level 1 = gather/recall · Level 2 = process/explain · Level 3 = apply/evaluate/imagine.

🤝 C: Talk Like a Pro

Your mission: You're in a tutorial group or Socratic Seminar. For each moment, choose the response that moves the group's thinking forward respectfully.

🗂️ O: SMART Goal Builder

Your mission: Build one SMART goal for this grading period. Each letter lights up when that part is strong.
